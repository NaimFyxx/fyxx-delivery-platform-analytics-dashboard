# Assessment

Ten of thirteen clean, and the two structural fixes that mattered most are properly done rather than patched around:

- the nav was moved out of `<main>` so `sticky` finally engages
- `--series-1` was decoupled from `--primary` / `--foreground`, which was the root cause behind two separate identical-colour bugs across the last three audits

The two failures share a shape: both are the last mile of a fix that was otherwise completed. The buttons got their 44px and then got hidden. The pace bar got its cream foreground everywhere except three info icons.

Item 4 is the one worth pushing back on, because it is not an unfinished fix, it is a change that made the nav worse in exchange for vertical space you had already won elsewhere.

## Suggested next steps

1. **Move Add product, Merge item and Export CSV out of the Filters panel.** They are page actions, not filters. A row above the table, or a single overflow menu, either works.
2. **Fix the three info icons in the pace bar.** One token swap, `#030C0C` to the same cream the rest of the bar uses.
3. **Reconsider the logo in the nav strip.** Putting it back above the tabs, or dropping it on mobile entirely, restores four visible tabs for about 30px. Two of five sections visible is worse than the 57px it bought.
4. **Decide about the orphan dot** on the single-month rate series: draw it with a legend entry, or do not draw it.
5. **Optional, for the last 47px:** move "2 months to review" and the stale badge onto the nav row and delete the sub-header.
